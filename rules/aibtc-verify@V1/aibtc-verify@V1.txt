units   : all amounts are integers in the smallest unit of AIBTC (1 AIBTC = 100000000 units)

pack    : the records whose sequence is within eventSeqRange of a pack manifest; pack_number = that manifest's epoch
final   : a pack is FINALIZED when AiBTC marks its Bitcoin anchor final, at 6 confirmations or more
round   : a pack opens when it is FINALIZED; it closes when, after it opened, the first pack of the same log with a larger pack_number becomes FINALIZED
public  : a record is public if AiBTC's public record interface serves its canonicalPayload to anonymous readers under the publication rule in force when the pack opens
          a failed or timed-out read does not make a record non-public
head    = the public record with the lowest sequence in the pack; tail = the public record with the highest
          one public record: head = tail; no public record: the pack has no verification and no release
          which records are public, and so head and tail, are fixed when the pack opens and stay the same for the whole round
claim(rec, A)   = SHA256(T2 ‖ T2 ‖ previous_event_hash[32] ‖ sequence[u64 BE] ‖ len(P)[u32 BE] ‖ P ‖ len(A)[u32 BE] ‖ A)
                  T2 = SHA256("AIBTC/VerifyClaim/v1"); A = the account's full login address, UTF-8
                  P = the UTF-8 bytes of the canonicalPayload string value, after decoding the interface's JSON once, with no further escaping
                  every [32] field is decoded from the lowercase hex exactly as the public interface gives it, without reversing bytes
verify(pack, A) = SHA256(T3 ‖ T3 ‖ pack_number[u64 BE] ‖ anchor_block_hash[32] ‖ record_count[u32 BE] ‖ claim(head, A) ‖ claim(tail, A))
                  T3 = SHA256("AIBTC/VerifyBatch/v1"); record_count = all records in the pack, public or not
                  T2, T3 and each claim enter as raw 32-byte digests; head = tail: one claim; only the result is written as 64 lowercase hex
snapshot        : one fixed ledger snapshot, taken after the round closes and before its first payout is credited; R and every shares(a) are read from it
release         = floor(R × r); R = available AIBTC balance of pool in the snapshot
shares(a)       = floor(available platform AIBTC balance of a in the snapshot / H), for accounts that submitted verify(pack, A) while the pack was open
per_share       = floor(release / S), S = sum of shares; payout(a) = shares(a) × per_share; remainders stay in pool
                  S = 0: the pack is settled with zero accounts and zero shares; nothing is released
list/v1         : one line per account with shares(a) > 0: the compact JSON array ["login address","shares","payout"], no spaces
                  shares and payout are decimal strings without leading zeros (zero is "0"); payout is in units
                  lines are sorted by the UTF-8 bytes of the login address, ascending; each line ends with LF; UTF-8, no BOM; an empty list is zero bytes
settlement      : payout(a) is the planned amount; it is credited to a's platform balance; the amount actually credited is what the completed ledger posting shows
                  each settlement with S > 0 is a public record citing the SHA-256 of this text, the sequences of head and tail, the SHA-256 of list/v1,
                  and any planned amount that was not credited
